## Purpose

Behavioural standards for VUUSH people, partners, and systems under pressure. Values are **decision filters** — not posters.

They express the Brand Pillars in human conduct:

> Commit clearly · Complete faithfully · Return time · Stay precise · Serve the whole ecosystem

---

## 1. How we use values

| Use | Expectation |
|-----|-------------|
| Hiring | Prefer people who stay calm, exact, and accountable |
| Performance | Reward faithful completion and honesty under stress — not heroics |
| Product & ops | When trade-offs appear, values + Brand Foundation decide |
| Partners | We do not adopt partners who require us to lie to users |

---

## 2. The values

### 2.1 Clarity

**We make commitment obvious.**

Soft intent and hard commitment are never blurred. People always know what is draft and what is real.

| Behaviours | Anti-behaviours |
|------------|-----------------|
| Name the decision; state the consequence | Soft language on hard commits |
| Precise states in product and ops | “It’s basically done” when it isn’t |
| Ask “where is The Point?” | Shipping ambiguity to move faster |

---

### 2.2 Accountability

**After commitment, we own completion.**

We do not abandon work in silence. We finish, recover, or tell the truth about the block.

| Behaviours | Anti-behaviours |
|------------|-----------------|
| Clear ownership after accept/confirm/pay | Orphaned jobs and unanswered exceptions |
| Honest status when stuck | Fake progress to reduce tickets |
| Close the loop | “Not my side of the app” |

---

### 2.3 Respect for time

**We give time back; we do not burn it.**

Attention is valuable. We subtract friction before we add features.

| Behaviours | Anti-behaviours |
|------------|-----------------|
| Fewer steps; clearer defaults | Urgency theatre and noise |
| Meetings and copy that earn their minutes | Process for process’s sake |
| Automate only when it returns time safely | Cleverness that creates rework |

---

### 2.4 Calm precision

**We stay composed and exact — especially under load.**

Premium means refined and reliable, not flashy. Panic is not a culture.

| Behaviours | Anti-behaviours |
|------------|-----------------|
| Steady language and systems in incidents | Blame theatre; shouting UI |
| Evidence over ego | Hotfixes that hide root cause |
| Craft in the details users feel | Sloppy “ship now, truth later” |

---

### 2.5 Integrity

**We tell the truth even when it is uncomfortable.**

No invented ETAs, no decorative danger, no comforting fiction.

| Behaviours | Anti-behaviours |
|------------|-----------------|
| “We don’t know yet” when we don’t | Fake certainty |
| Correct errors in the open | Quietly rewriting history |
| Align customer, driver, and ops truth | Different stories for different roles |

---

### 2.6 One ecosystem

**Every role deserves the same standard of care.**

Drivers and operators are not second-class. Customers are not the only humans who matter.

| Behaviours | Anti-behaviours |
|------------|-----------------|
| Dignity in driver and ops tools | “They’ll figure it out” |
| Shared truth across apps | Pretty consumer / chaotic back office |
| Design for stress and thumb | Neglecting the people who complete the work |

---

### 2.7 Long-term craft

**We build a company that can outgrow logistics without losing its soul.**

Short-term hacks that poison trust are not wins.

| Behaviours | Anti-behaviours |
|------------|-----------------|
| Decisions that still make sense in AI / enterprise chapters | Courier-only traps that block the future |
| Atlas discipline — company before careless code | Parallel truths and shadow docs |
| Improve in place | Forking culture |

---

## 3. When values compete

Default resolution order under conflict:

1. **Integrity** (truth)  
2. **Accountability** (completion after The Point)  
3. **Clarity** (commitment must stay obvious)  
4. **Respect for time** & **Calm precision**  
5. **One ecosystem**  
6. **Long-term craft**

Escalate to the owner when a choice would violate Integrity or Accountability for speed.
