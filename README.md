## Purpose

Project Atlas is the single source of truth for VUUSH. It holds the company before the code: vision, brand, product, software standards, operations, go-to-market, investors, legal, and enterprise architecture.

We are a technology company. Intelligent logistics is our proving ground. We build calm, precise systems that connect customers, drivers, and businesses — and that can grow into AI, automation, and enterprise software without losing our soul.

**Brand promise:** VUUSH gives people their time back by reducing the time between intention and completion.

## How to Use Atlas

1. Read Foundation before proposing product or technical work.  
2. Treat each chapter Status line as authoritative.  
3. Architecture phases are gated — do not open the next phase without approval.  
4. Where an approved architecture phase and a handbook outline differ, the approved phase wins until handbook sync.  
5. Improve documents in place; do not fork parallel truths.  
6. Move superseded material to `08_ARCHIVE` per policy.
