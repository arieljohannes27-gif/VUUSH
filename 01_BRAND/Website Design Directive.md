## Authority

This document governs the **public VUUSH website** — the front door to the ecosystem.

It is **not** the product UI. Product surfaces follow Design System / Visual Principles. Where website taste and product UI conflict, each follows its own authority in its own surface.

---

## Design Principle #1 (Atlas)

> **VUUSH is remembered not for what it adds, but for what it has the discipline to leave out.**

Challenge every design decision: *Does this reduce friction?* If not, remove it.

---

## What this site is

The reception area of a world-class technology company.

Calm. Quiet. Confident. Premium. Never loud.

Think Apple · Linear · Notion · OpenAI — not Uber · Bolt · courier marketplaces.

---

## Three responsibilities only

1. **Explain who VUUSH is** — the company, in one clear sentence. Not every feature.  
2. **Help visitors choose their path** — Customer · Driver · Enterprise.  
3. **Build trust** — through restraint. No hype, clutter, or fake urgency.

---

## Homepage structure (locked)

1. **Hero** — VUUSH · one purpose statement · one short support line · three actions  
2. **The Philosophy** — The Point, lightly  
3. **Products** — Customer · Driver · Enterprise · API · AI (future)  
4. **About** — Mission · Vision · Leadership · Story · Careers · Technology  
5. **Footer** — simple, elegant  

---

## Reject

Uber/Bolt clones · delivery clichés (trucks, parcels, map wallpaper) · stats theatre · neon · busy dashboards · feature overload · corporate stock · “fastest delivery” urgency.
